Work. Take a short reset. Return.

Reset lives in your Mac's menu bar and counts the time you actually spend working. Before a long stretch runs on, it offers a short break: 20 seconds for your eyes, two minutes to move, ten to step away. Then it hands you back to your work.

It counts your work, not the clock

Reset measures active time only. Step away to make coffee or take a call away from the desk, and Reset notices: a natural rest counts as a rest, and the timer starts over.

Three rhythms work together, and every interval and length is yours to change. The default follows the familiar 20-20-20 pattern for tired eyes.

It knows when not to interrupt

A break in the middle of a call helps no one. Reset holds its breaks by itself, and tells you plainly what it is waiting for.

  • While another app is using the microphone
  • While a video, a presentation, or a screen share keeps the display awake
  • During fullscreen work
  • While Zoom, Microsoft Teams, FaceTime, or an app you choose is in front
  • For a pause in your typing, so a break never lands mid-sentence

Meeting mode holds breaks for 30, 60, or 120 minutes. Working hours keep Reset quiet in the evening and at the weekend.

Twenty-six small routines, and one patient cat

Each break brings one simple thing to do and a quiet countdown. Reset's cat shows the movement, so you never have to read instructions mid-break.

Eyes, shoulders, neck, hands, posture, breathing, movement, sit-stand, and longer rests. One switch keeps every routine seated, you choose which kinds appear, and you can start any of them yourself.

Your day, in plain words

Today tells you three things that matter: how long since your last rest, how many breaks you took, and your longest stretch without one. Then the minutes you worked each hour, in a chart that needs no legend.

Your next reset, on your desktop

With Reset Pro, a widget shows the work left before your next reset and today's three facts, without opening anything.

Private by design

Reset is a break coach, not a tracker. Everything it knows about your day stays on your Mac.

No account, no ads

There is nothing to sign up for, and nothing is sold or shown to you but your own breaks.

Nothing about your work

Reset never records app names, window titles, what you type, or what is on your screen.

No camera, no microphone

Reset never opens either. To hold a break during a call, it only asks macOS whether a microphone is in use.

AI only if you ask

Optional Gemini personalization is off until you turn it on, and sends only rounded totals and counts. Never your apps or your words.
