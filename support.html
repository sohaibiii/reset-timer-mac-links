Getting started

What do I need to run Reset?

A Mac with macOS 14 Sonoma or later, Apple silicon or Intel. Reset works fully offline; it needs the internet only if you turn on cloud personalization.

Where is Reset after I open it?

In the menu bar, at the top right of your screen. Reset has no Dock icon by design. Setup takes about a minute: choose a mode and a rhythm, decide about reminders and window awareness, and choose whether Reset opens when you log in.

If you can't see the icon, the menu bar may be full. Hide a few other menu-bar items and it will appear.

What is the difference between Gentle, Smart, and Strict?
  • Gentle sends a notification and waits for you to start the break.
  • Smart gives a short warning, then shows a calm full-screen break you can snooze or skip.
  • Strict, with Reset Pro, has no Snooze or Skip. Escape or Emergency exit always ends a break, in every mode.
Can I change how often breaks come?

Yes. In Settings, Break rhythm, you can change the interval and length of the micro, recovery, and long resets, and choose which kinds of routines appear. With Reset Pro you can keep several named rhythms and let them switch by the clock.

When breaks come, and when they wait

A break didn't cover the screen. Why?

Smart and Strict cover the screen only with window awareness on (Settings, Protection, Fullscreen). Without it they send a reminder with a Start button, as Gentle always does.

Reset also holds breaks on purpose: during working-hours limits, a pause, Meeting mode, a meeting app or fullscreen app in front, or while another app uses the microphone. The menu-bar window and Today always say what is holding the break.

Why did a break wait while I was on a call?

While another app is using a microphone, Reset holds the break, and gives its warning a little over a minute after the microphone is free and you are active again. Dictation or a recording app can count too. Reset never listens: it only asks macOS whether a microphone is in use. You can turn this off in Settings, Protection, Microphone and display.

Why did a break wait while I watched a video?

A video, a presentation, or a screen share usually keeps the display awake, and Reset waits while it does. After two hours of that without a gap, Reset stops waiting and sends reminders instead. A video that started in the last few seconds may not be noticed yet.

A calendar event held my break, but it isn't a meeting.

Choose "Not a meeting, resume" in the menu-bar window to let breaks through for that event. All-day events, events marked free, and events you declined never hold a break. You can choose which calendars count in Settings, Protection, Calendar.

A break came a few seconds after its warning ran out.

In Smart and Strict, a break waits for a pause in your typing or dragging before it covers the screen, for at most ten seconds, so it never lands mid-sentence. A break you start yourself, a Gentle reminder, and the break at the end of a focus session don't wait.

I was away, and no break came when I got back.

That is on purpose. Five, ten, or fifteen minutes without input, or with the Mac asleep, count as a rest and restart the micro, recovery, or long timer. The same happens after a pause, a night outside working hours, or reopening Reset after that long.

My break settings changed by themselves.

A rhythm with a schedule comes into use by itself during its hours. With more than one rhythm, the menu-bar window and Today say which is in use. A rhythm you choose by hand stays until the next scheduled change.

Reset Pro and purchases

What is free, and what does Reset Pro add?

The free app is the whole break coach: the timer, Gentle and Smart, all 26 routines, Today, every automatic hold, Meeting mode, working hours, and pause.

Reset Pro adds calendar awareness, named rhythms, Strict mode, focus sessions, History, What Reset did, Shortcuts and Focus filters, the desktop widget, and optional Gemini personalization.

What plans are there?
  • Monthly and yearly subscriptions, each with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers that you can cancel at no charge.
  • Reset Pro Lifetime, one payment, with no renewal and no trial.
How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Open the App Store, click your name, then Account Settings, Manage Subscriptions. Or in Reset, choose Settings, General, Manage in App Store. A cancelled plan keeps working until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I restore Reset Pro on another Mac?

Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account, then use Restore Purchases in Reset's Settings or on the plans sheet. It finds subscriptions and Lifetime alike. Family Sharing is not offered for Reset Pro.

I bought Reset Pro, but Reset still shows the plans.

Reset checks with the App Store every quarter of an hour, whenever it comes to the front, and whenever the plans sheet opens. To check at once, use Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account that bought it.

I subscribe and want Lifetime instead.

Buy Lifetime in Settings, General, where Reset offers it beside your subscription. Then cancel the subscription in App Store settings so it doesn't renew. Lifetime stays in force whatever happens to the subscription.

What happens when Reset Pro ends?

Each Pro feature locks again and nothing is lost: Strict switches to Smart, personalization turns off, and calendar holds stop. Your history, rhythms, calendars, and settings are kept, and come back if you subscribe again.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are made through Apple, so refunds are too. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Permissions

Which permissions does Reset need?

None are required. Notifications let Reset remind you gently; Calendar lets breaks wait for busy events. Reset asks for no Microphone, Camera, Accessibility, or Screen Recording permission. Reset's Settings show the live state of each permission and what to do next.

I declined Notifications or Calendar and want to allow them.
  • Notifications: System Settings, Notifications, Reset. Reset's Settings has a button that opens this pane.
  • Calendar: System Settings, Privacy & Security, Calendars, Reset. The Calendar row in Reset's Settings opens it.
Reset says "Context unknown, using gentle reminders".

When the front app has no window on screen, such as the desktop or an app with every window closed, Reset can't tell whether you are in fullscreen work, so it sends a reminder instead of covering the screen.

Something else

Cloud personalization says "not connected".

Turn it off and on again in Settings to register again, or read the status line for the reason. All break timing works without it.

A Shortcuts action says it can't run.

Reset's Shortcuts actions do what the same button in Reset does, and when Reset wouldn't offer that button they say why: before setup, during a break, while the Mac is locked, or with nothing to resume. Focus sessions and Strict mode need Reset Pro.

How do I delete my data?

In Settings, Personalization & privacy: Delete history removes your history and keeps your settings, and Reset all data removes everything, including your cloud registration. See the privacy policy.

Removing Reset

  1. If you used cloud personalization, choose Settings, Personalization & privacy, Reset all data first, so the service deletes your registration.
  2. Turn off Open at login in Reset's Settings, and remove Reset's widget if you added one.
  3. Quit Reset from its menu-bar window and move it from Applications to the Trash.
  4. To remove your history too, delete ~/Library/Containers/com.social.reset.
